News that matters

$2.7B leaves Binance hot wallets, hack unconfirmed

After the window closed, at 20:24 UTC, Binance's 'Binance 14' hot wallet sent about $2.53B in 64 tokens to a freshly created address in one transaction, and a second Binance hot wallet sent $174.4M to the same address 24 seconds later. The moves included about $1.67B USDT and 195,470 ETH. The call that started it came from an address funded through Tornado Cash, per PANews and onchain posts. Binance had not commented by 21:30 UTC.

Why it matters. CT is split. Spreek's 'possibly massive Binance hack' post passed 390K views. StarPlatinum says the flow looks more like an internal wallet migration, since the destination had not moved the funds and the source wallet kept operating. PANews reports a further 780M USDC from a third hot wallet, while StarPlatinum says that transfer did not complete. Walter Bloomberg reported Tether froze $971M USDT; Tether has not posted on it. In the hour that followed, $545M in positions were liquidated, almost all longs (CoinGlass).

If you hold or use this: Using Binance: there is no official word yet. Watch Binance's own channels and do not trust 'refund' or 'recovery' links.

Sources: Spreek on X, PANews, PANews (780M USDC), StarPlatinum on X, Walter Bloomberg on X, Etherscan tx

NYSE owner ICE brings Hyperliquid perps onshore

ICE will be the exclusive operator of a new US retail market and clearing structure for Trade[XYZ]'s Hyperliquid-based perpetual futures, covering crypto, equities, commodities and FX, subject to regulatory approval. ICE is taking a minority stake in Trade[XYZ]. No launch date was given. Customers would reach it through futures brokers and partner platforms, including the ICE-OKX venture.

Why it matters. Stan Druckenmiller's first ever X post said he is an investor in Trade[XYZ] and drew 4.8M views. Elon Musk replied 'Interesting' (3.1M views). ICE CEO Jeff Sprecher called the perps additive to ICE's futures markets. HYPE ran from about $80 to $93 over the window, per CoinGecko.

If you hold or use this: Holding HYPE: launch depends on CFTC approval and has no date. Trade[XYZ]'s existing markets on Hyperliquid keep running (CoinDesk).

Sources: Druckenmiller on X, ICE press release, The Block, CoinDesk, Decrypt, Elon Musk on X

Singapore police: tampered Ledgers sold in local stores

Singapore police said some Ledger hardware wallets bought from retail stores in Singapore contained malicious hardware components that could capture recovery phrases or private keys. At least two cases since 10 Sept have cost about S$3.5M. Ledger is reviewing the cases and investigations continue.

Why it matters. Wu Blockchain's post drew 162K views and CZ's warning to Singapore users 243K. Police advise anyone who suspects a tampered device to stop using it and move funds to a new seed set up on a trusted device.

If you hold or use this: Own a Ledger bought from a third-party shop: check Ledger's official advisories and consider moving to a fresh seed on a device bought direct.

Sources: Wu Blockchain on X, Singapore Police Force, CZ on X

ED searches Mudrex and Instadapp in ₹5,000 crore probe

India's Enforcement Directorate searched premises linked to Mudrex and Saber.money (RPFAS Technologies) and Instadapp on 27 Oct under FEMA, and announced preliminary findings on 29 Oct. It alleges USDT and USDC were used to move money abroad outside authorised channels, with more than ₹5,000 crore (about $525M) involved, per crypto.news.

Why it matters. The ED puts the Mudrex side above ₹4,600 crore and Instadapp at about $40M, and names Binance and Bybit as platforms used. It calls the findings preliminary. No arrests or penalties were announced.

If you hold or use this: Mudrex user: no withdrawal deadline or freeze was announced. Watch for an official statement from the company.

Sources: crypto.news, Deccan Herald

Polymarket pauses after a $2.7M front-end drain

After the window, at 18:33 UTC, Polymarket said a third-party provider for its international site was compromised, halted trading and referred the case to law enforcement. It resumed around 19:00 UTC and says affected traders will be fully reimbursed. DefiLlama's 0xngmi put losses at about $2.7M, per Bitcoin.com News.

Why it matters. 0xngmi said the attacker hit a web performance tracking company whose scripts loaded on Polymarket. CZ called it a front-end injection that shows a valid transaction while asking users to sign a different one. Polymarket's post drew 338K views.

If you hold or use this: Polymarket user: check recent signatures and approvals, and expect reimbursement per Polymarket.

Sources: Polymarket on X, Bitcoin.com News, 0xngmi on X

zkLink Nova bridge drained for about $780k

zkLink Nova's Ethereum bridge was exploited for about $780k through a forged batch root sent via a public relayer (Blockaid, SlowMist). The team confirmed it, paused production and offered a 20% white-hat bounty. DefiLlama shows a preliminary $783k.

Why it matters. Funds went to the attacker and recovery is uncertain (Grok).

If you hold or use this: Using zkLink Nova: stay away until the team confirms a fix.

Sources: The Defiant

One thing to learn

Perpetual futures. Perps are leveraged contracts that track an asset's price with no expiry date. Funding payments move between longs and shorts to keep the contract near spot. ICE running Hyperliquid-style perps in a CFTC-regulated US market could widen access to the onchain engine underneath (Grok).
