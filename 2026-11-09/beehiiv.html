News that matters

$1B in longs wiped in a Sunday-night flush

At 22:04 UTC on 8 Nov, Watcher.Guru reported $1B liquidated in 10 minutes as BTC fell under $74k and ETH under $2,125. BTC dropped from $75,690 to a low near $73,500 in about 15 minutes (Bitcoin.com). CoinGlass counts $1.21B liquidated over the 24h to 16:52 UTC 9 Nov, 87% of it longs. ETH led with $509M, ahead of BTC at $325M. The largest single order was a $48.16M ETH long on Binance, closed at $2,095.50.

Why it matters. Reports point to the Nvidia CEO scandal, stalled US-Iran talks and thin weekend liquidity, with crypto the only big market open (Bitcoin.com, CoinCentral). ETH also carries ETF selling: spot ETH ETFs lost $363M over 7 days and BTC ETFs $1.32B (Lookonchain). One trader's 56,667 ETH long, about $118M, was fully liquidated for a $44.78M loss (Lookonchain via crypto.news). Watcher.Guru's post drew 1.9M views, the biggest crypto post of the window.

If you hold or use this: Trading with leverage: weekend liquidity is thin. Size down before Sunday nights.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, CoinGlass liquidations, Bitcoin.com, CoinCentral, crypto.news

Coinbase gets its federal trust bank charter

The OCC gave final approval to Coinbase National Trust Company, upgrading the conditional approval from April. It moves Coinbase's institutional custody business under one federal regulator. Coinbase says it will not take retail deposits or run fractional reserves (Decrypt).

Why it matters. Coinbase joins Circle (July) and Ripple (October), whose trust banks back USDC and RLUSD reserves. Block and Kraken's parent Payward have applications pending. Senator Warren calls the OCC's crypto charters illegal, and community bankers are suing the OCC (Decrypt). Watcher.Guru's post drew 805K views.

If you hold or use this: Coinbase retail user: nothing changes for you.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, Decrypt

Blumenthal gives Binance, Tether and WLFI until 4 Jan

Senator Richard Blumenthal, top Democrat on the Senate Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations, sent follow-up letters on 5 Nov to Binance, Tether, World Liberty Financial and Fight Fight Fight, the firm behind the TRUMP memecoin. All four have until 4 Jan 2027 to hand over records (CoinCentral). Firms were asked to start producing records by 30 Nov (FinanceFeeds).

Why it matters. The requests are voluntary for now. Blumenthal is expected to chair the subcommittee when the new Congress meets on 3 Jan, and the chair can issue subpoenas. The probes cover Iran-linked flows through Binance and USDT, and conflicts of interest in Trump family crypto ventures. Binance calls the Iran allegations false and Tether says it backed about $550M in Iran-linked freezes this year (CoinCentral, FinanceFeeds). Watcher.Guru's post drew 658K views.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, CoinCentral, FinanceFeeds

Strategy buys 1,036 BTC, now holds 850,364

Strategy bought 1,036 BTC for $81.9M at about $79,012 each between 2 and 8 Nov, and repurchased $188.3M of STRC. Both were funded by selling about $283.8M of MSTR common stock. It now holds 850,364 BTC at an average cost of $75,456 (Bitcoin Magazine, 8-K).

Why it matters. BTC closed the window at $75,041, about 0.5% under Strategy's average cost. Saylor's post drew 313K views.

Sources: Michael Saylor on X, Bitcoin Magazine

NYSE picks Avalanche to settle tokenized stocks

Avalanche will provide the core ledger for the clearing firm of NYSE's upcoming tokenized securities platform, an alternative trading system built for 24/7 trading, clearing and settlement, subject to regulatory approvals (NYSE, Ava Labs). NYSE will run its own chain built on Avalanche's technology, separate from the public Avalanche chain (CoinDesk).

Why it matters. The platform pairs NYSE's Pillar trading engine with on-chain clearing, settlement and custody, and aims for stablecoin funding. No launch date was given. Nasdaq and DTCC are building their own tokenization rails (CoinDesk). AVAX rose 7.3% in 24h (CoinGecko).

Sources: NYSE on X, Ava Labs release, CoinDesk, The Block

Schwab adds SOL, AVAX and LINK

Charles Schwab added Solana, Avalanche and Chainlink to Schwab Crypto accounts, next to BTC and ETH. Trades cost 75 basis points. Accounts are open in every US state except New York and Louisiana (Schwab).

Why it matters. Schwab says more assets will follow over time. Watcher.Guru's post drew 266K views.

If you hold or use this: Schwab Crypto client: the three new coins are live in your account.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, Schwab press release

Binance hackers launder through homemade memecoins

Elliptic says North Korean launderers created at least six throwaway tokens on NEAR between 5 and 7 Nov, each paired with NEAR in its own pool, and pushed about 117,000 NEAR ($430K) through them. Each token cost about 25 NEAR to create. Stolen funds never moved directly between wallets. Similar tricks showed up on Tron on 2 Nov and Solana on 3 Nov.

Why it matters. About 11.8M NEAR ($43.7M) from the theft had not moved yet. Elliptic tells compliance teams to stop treating liquidity-pool funds as clean by default and to watch for new tokens with few traders. The story trended on X.

Sources: Elliptic, crypto.news

Ankr's Ethereum bridge loses $303K

An attacker pulled about $303,600 from Ankr's Ethereum bridge in five withdrawals that referenced a small Optimism deposit that had already been redeemed (The Defiant, preliminary).

Why it matters. The contract was still unpaused at 08:49 UTC, and Ankr had not commented (The Defiant). Fund status is unclear.

If you hold or use this: Ankr bridge user: stay off the bridge until Ankr confirms a fix.

Sources: The Defiant, The Crypto Times
