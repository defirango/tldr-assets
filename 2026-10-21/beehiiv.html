News that matters

Alpenglow goes live on Solana mainnet

Solana validators switched to Alpenglow at slot 459,653,000, around 14:00 UTC on 21 Oct, per Anza. It retires TowerBFT, which Solana had run since its March 2020 launch, and condenses transaction confirmation into a single step. Anza says finality drops from about 12.8 seconds to about 150ms.

Why it matters. Anza CEO Brennan Watt called it the largest, most ambitious blockchain upgrade since the Merge. Faster finality lets exchanges credit deposits and merchants settle payments sooner. Rotor, a change to how block data moves, comes later with no date set, per The Block.

If you hold or use this: Running a Solana app or validator: check validator status and RPC after the switch.

Sources: Solana on X, The Block, Solana Foundation release

Zcash sets NU7 for around 5 Nov, ZEC +10.8%

Zcash Improvement Proposal editors agreed on block 3,543,405, expected around 5 Nov, for the NU7 upgrade, per CoinDesk. Block time drops from 75 to 25 seconds, so a three-confirmation wait falls to about a minute and a quarter. Sprout, Zcash's original privacy pool, shuts down at activation.

Why it matters. Coins left in Sprout become unspendable. 60% of each block's fees leave circulation for now and are paid back to miners from about February 2031, and the halving schedule stays the same. ZEC rose 10.8% to $1,333.77 and is trending on CoinGecko.

If you hold or use this: Holding ZEC in Sprout: move it out before activation in early November.

Sources: CoinDesk, CoinDesk markets

SpaceX and Tesla explore a merger with 30,221 BTC

Elon Musk said on SpaceX's Q3 earnings call on 20 Oct that both companies have formed special committees of independent directors to assess a tie-up. The committees have not agreed on terms, he said. SpaceX holds 18,712 BTC and Tesla 11,509 BTC, per CoinDesk.

Why it matters. A deal would put 30,221 BTC on one balance sheet. CoinDesk reports no plan to sell. SpaceX shares rose about 9% premarket after a revenue beat, and Tesla reports after Wednesday's US close.

Sources: CoinDesk, KuCoin News

OCC finalizes the first GENIUS Act rule

The OCC issued a final rule implementing the GENIUS Act on 21 Oct. It covers payment stablecoin issuance, reserves, redemptions, risk management, audits and supervision, plus crypto custody at federally supervised institutions, per crypto.news. The act takes effect on 18 Jan 2027.

Why it matters. From that date, issuers generally need federal authorization. Issuers with substantially complete applications pending may request waivers of up to 12 months. AML and sanctions rules sit in a separate OCC proposal from June. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called it a watershed moment.

Sources: The Block, crypto.news
