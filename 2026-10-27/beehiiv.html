News that matters

Ripple gets its bank charter and signs Robinhood

The OCC gave final approval on 26 Oct for Ripple National Trust Bank. It will manage and hold in trust the segregated reserves behind RLUSD, now a $2.5B stablecoin, and later offer digital asset custody. RLUSD issuance stays with Standard Custody & Trust under NYDFS, per Ripple. The bank is not open yet.

Why it matters. Ripple followed up on 27 Oct. Robinhood will use RLUSD on the XRP Ledger for institutional settlement, run its treasury on Ripple Treasury, and keep Ripple Prime financing on Bitstamp. RLUSD is live on Robinhood Chain and Bitstamp now and reaches the Robinhood app later in 2026. Ripple also widened its BNY tie-up across custody and tokenized trade finance. Garlinghouse's post drew 1.1M views and Watcher.Guru's 1.7M.

If you hold or use this: Holding XRP: the token rose 2.1% on the day and is still down 1.8% on the week (CoinGecko). The charter covers reserves and custody only.

Sources: Brad Garlinghouse on X, Ripple press release, Ripple and Robinhood (Business Wire), Ripple on X (Robinhood), Ripple on X (BNY), Watcher.Guru on X

One thing to learn

National trust bank charter. An OCC national trust charter lets a crypto firm hold stablecoin reserves and offer custody under federal supervision. It does not allow deposit taking or lending like a full commercial bank, and it does not come with a Federal Reserve master account by default. Issuance can stay under existing state oversight (Grok).
