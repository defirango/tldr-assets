Why it matters. Hours earlier, New York Fed President John Williams said one more hike this year may be appropriate, per CoinDesk. Hotter prices and higher yields leave less room for risk assets.

Alpenglow goes live on 21 Oct

Anza says Alpenglow activates on Solana mainnet-beta on Wednesday 21 October at about 14:50 UTC. Feature gate SIMD-0384 starts a 5,000-slot countdown, then validators move to the new Votor voting system, per Unchained. Finality drops from about 12.8 seconds to about 150 ms. Validators must run Agave 4.3.0, and Firedancer and Frankendancer are not supported for the first migration, per CoinDesk. The roughly 1 SOL per epoch voting cost ends, and validators pay a flat 0.8 SOL admission fee.

Why it matters. Votes leave the blocks, so reported TPS can drop sharply even if user activity stays the same, per CoinDesk. Expect that chart to get misread.

If you hold or use this: Running a validator: upgrade to Agave 4.3.0 before 21 Oct.

Sources: Anza on X, CoinDesk, Unchained, Anza: Alpenglow

Coinbase wants Bitcoin's quantum prep started now

Coinbase, which says it holds about 5% of all BTC for clients, published its input on post-quantum readiness on 13 October. It wants output formats that can swap in new signature schemes, naming BIP-360 (P2MR) and P2TRv2. It backs hybrid 1-of-2 wallets with a classic key and a post-quantum key as a first stage, a stateless signature option and MPC-friendly schemes. Dormant and lost coins should be handled as a separate debate, and any rescue path must itself be quantum-safe.

Why it matters. Coinbase says consensus changes and migrations can take years, so work should start well ahead of any quantum computer announcement. CoinDesk cites Google researchers projecting one could exist by 2029.

Sources: Coinbase blog, CoinDesk

Unichain moves to OP Enterprise

Uniswap's Unichain started its testnet migration to OP Enterprise on 13 October, and mainnet follows on 29 October. Optimism takes over sequencers, RPC, monitoring and incident response. Chain ID, contracts, balances and apps stay the same, and Unichain stays a Stage 1 rollup. OP rose about 18% on 13 Oct per Crypto Times, and is down 3.3% over the last 24h on CoinGecko.

Why it matters. It adds a big name to Optimism's managed chain service. Native interop with other OP Stack chains comes later, starting with ETH.

Sources: Uniswap blog, Crypto Times

One thing to learn

Alpenglow. Alpenglow replaces Solana's TowerBFT voting. Validator votes move out of blocks and into certificates, and a block finalizes after one or two voting rounds. Finality drops from about 12.8 seconds to about 150 ms. Reported TPS will fall because vote transactions no longer count, even if user activity stays flat. The roughly 1 SOL per epoch voting cost ends, and validators pay a flat 0.8 SOL admission fee.
