News that matters

Ripple's RLUSD goes live on Coinbase

From 28 Oct, retail and institutional clients can trade and manage RLUSD on Coinbase Prime, Coinbase Exchange and the Coinbase app, with 1:1 conversion from US dollars and other supported USD stablecoins. Deposits and withdrawals work on the XRP Ledger, Base and Ethereum, and Ripple will provide liquidity on Base. RLUSD is above $2.4B in market cap, per Ripple.

Why it matters. It lands a day after the Robinhood deal. The Block also reported an investment-grade Moody's rating for RLUSD and a $900M annualized product revenue run rate Ripple expects. Ripple's post drew 270K views and Watcher.Guru's 266K. XRP still only moved 0.5% on the day (CoinGecko).

If you hold or use this: Holding RLUSD: Coinbase Global Exchange access, including use as collateral, comes later per Ripple. Check which network you deposit on.

Sources: Ripple on X, Ripple press release, The Block, Watcher.Guru on X

Thailand's Supreme Court: Bitcoin is property

The court upheld a robbery conviction over 2.480801 BTC taken on Koh Samui in September 2022, worth about $50,000 at the time. The defendant argued Bitcoin has no intrinsic value and so cannot be stolen. The full bench rejected that in ruling 4929/2569, saying Bitcoin has market value, can be owned and can be exchanged for cash, per crypto.news.

Why it matters. The 10-year sentence stands, and the lower courts ordered the BTC returned or 1.878 million baht paid. It was the most-viewed crypto headline in the window at 471K views on Watcher.Guru.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, crypto.news, PANews

The Fed holds, and December hike odds climb

After the window closed, the Fed held rates at 3.75% to 4% in a 10-2 vote. Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissented in favor of a quarter-point hike. Why it matters. Warsh said more information will help set policy, which CoinDesk read as a December hike being less than certain. He also said the FOMC will meet eight times in 2027, with one meeting set aside for broad policy and no rate vote. BTC traded near $81.1k during the press conference.

Polymarket US adds 5-minute crypto markets

Polymarket's US app now lists 5-minute up/down markets on BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP. Each one asks only whether the price ends higher or lower after five minutes. That is 12 windows an hour and up to 288 a day per asset, per FinanceFeeds. Watcher.Guru's post drew 255K views.

Why it matters. The US app added 15-minute, hourly and daily markets on 15 Oct. Coin Edition, citing Reuters, says the CFTC is reviewing whether Polymarket kept its 2022 agreement to keep US users off the international site.

If you hold or use this: Trading them: Mayne's math says 17 straight doubles turns $10 into a million. The flip side is how fast a balance goes to zero.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, FinanceFeeds, Coin Edition

Hyperliquid perps go live in the Pump.fun app

Pump.fun says traders can now long or short crypto, stocks and commodities on Hyperliquid straight from their USDC balance in its app, with no bridging. Its post drew 211K views.

Why it matters. Pump.fun first added BTC and ETH perps through Hyperliquid builder codes on 16 Oct, with HyperCore handling matching and liquidations, per Bankless. This widens it to stocks and commodities a day after trade.xyz listed a Nifty 50 perp.

If you hold or use this: Bankless reported the perps are blocked in the US and Ontario. Check the builder fee before you trade.

Sources: Pump.fun on X, Bankless

Google unveils crypto custody for banks

Google Multi-Party Compute splits a signing key into shares held by separate parties, with thresholds such as 2-of-3 or 5-of-7, so the full key is never assembled in one place. It is in private preview with financial institutions and launches commercially in early 2027, per Bitcoin Magazine citing CoinDesk.

Why it matters. Solana says Alphabet is already securing its own digital assets on Solana with it. Google steps into a market led by Fireblocks, Fordefi and BitGo.

Sources: Solana on X, Bitcoin Magazine

Coinbase vendor leak and a Bithumb breach

Coinbase says it has not had a data breach. An incident at analytics vendor Amplitude exposed contact data for a small subset of Coinbase customers, and funds, passwords and keys were not affected, per CoinGape. SlowMist's CISO claimed more than 70,000 users were hit. Coinbase has not confirmed a number.

Why it matters. In Korea, Bithumb confirmed unauthorized access to an internal groupware system that leaked staff and partner data. It is still checking whether customer data was exposed, and the FSC opened an on-site probe, per Seoul Economic Daily. Grok also flagged Fence Finance at $1.01M from a settlement flaw (DefiLlama).

If you hold or use this: Coinbase or Bithumb user: treat any call or email about your account as a phishing attempt and log in only through the app.

Sources: CoinGape, Seoul Economic Daily, DefiLlama hacks
