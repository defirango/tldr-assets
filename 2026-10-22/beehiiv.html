Robinhood wins Dubai approval for crypto

The Dubai Financial Services Authority approved Robinhood for retail trading and custody across 10 asset classes, including bitcoin and other crypto, equities, ETFs and investment tokens, per Bitcoin Magazine. Robinhood will run its Middle East and North Africa business from the Dubai International Financial Centre and plans to launch in 2027.

Why it matters. Salah Kaddoura is president of Robinhood MENA. CEO Vlad Tenev called the DIFC one of the world's most important financial hubs. Robinhood has not said which countries it will serve at launch or how it will define eligible investors. Watcher.Guru's post on the approval drew 200K views.

If you hold or use this: Based in the Gulf: nothing to sign up for yet. The launch is set for 2027.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, Bitcoin Magazine, The National

China moves to confiscate 61,800 BTC

Tianjin's Second Intermediate People's Court accepted prosecutors' request to confiscate more than 61,800 BTC and other assets tied to Tianjin Lantian Gerui, per CoinDesk and crypto.news. The firm raised 40.378B yuan, about $5.7B, from more than 102,000 investors between 2014 and 2017. Its controller, Qian Zhimin, fled to the UK, where police seized the coins in 2018.

Why it matters. Victims have six months to join the Chinese case. In London, Justice Mark Turner ruled on 12 Oct that Chinese law governs the victims' ownership claims to the coins, and some victims are appealing, per crypto.news. Qian was sentenced in the UK to 11 years and 8 months for money laundering.

Sources: CoinDesk, crypto.news

Tesla still holds 11,509 BTC

Tesla held 11,509 BTC at the end of Q3, unchanged from the prior quarter, per Bloomingbit. It booked $288M in unrealized gains on digital assets before tax, and the carrying value rose about 42.7% to $962M.

Why it matters. The same earnings report added to the AI-spending worries behind Thursday's sell-off, per CoinDesk. SpaceX holds another 18,712 BTC, and the two companies are exploring a merger.

Sources: Bloomingbit, CoinDesk

One thing to learn

Alpenglow finality. Alpenglow replaces Solana's TowerBFT with a new consensus that reaches about 150ms finality by aggregating stake-weighted votes off-chain into certificates. Transactions become irreversible much faster, so counterparties can treat transfers as settled with less delay, which matters for payments and trading (Grok).
