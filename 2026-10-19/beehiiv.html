Qubic halts its chain after a wallet drain

An attacker drained 1,404 Qubic wallets of about 2.94 trillion QU between 23:42 UTC on 17 Oct and 01:26 UTC on 18 Oct, per Qubic. That is roughly $1.3M to $1.4M, per BeInCrypto and CoinGape. The seeds were generated with too little randomness, and Qubic says the protocol itself was unaffected. Computors halted the network at tick 85,371,949.

Why it matters. 87.1% of the drained funds never left Qubic and stay frozen by the halt. MEXC froze the account that received the other 12.9%. The fix shipped in wallet v4.26.4. No restart date is set, and the Computor quorum decides any recovery. Exchanges suspended QUBIC deposits and withdrawals.

If you hold or use this: Holding QUBIC: seeds made on iPhone, iPad or Mac Safari before 18 Oct are affected. Ignore anyone offering paid recovery.

Sources: Qubic on X, BeInCrypto, CoinGape

JPM Coin goes live on Ethereum

JPMorgan's Kinexys brought JPM Coin (JPMD), its USD deposit token, to Ethereum for institutional clients, per a statement shared with The Block. JPMD launched on Base in November 2025 and now supports peer-to-peer transfers across Ethereum and Base.

Why it matters. Kinexys says it has handled over $4T in cumulative transactions, with about $7B a day on average. JPMorgan launched its first tokenized money-market fund on Ethereum in December 2025.

Sources: The Block, JPM Coin

Arbitrum pays 2M ARB for USDG growth

The Arbitrum Foundation's Stablecoin Growth Program, announced 15 Oct, pays 200K ARB each to the first 10 teams that reach $10M of USDG TVL on a 30-day average. Applicants need at least $1M in stablecoin balances and must make USDG their main Arbitrum stablecoin.

Why it matters. ARB rose 9.5% to $0.196 on CoinGecko today, and Grok ties the move to the program.

If you hold or use this: Building a stablecoin app on Arbitrum: check eligibility and apply through the Foundation's form.

Sources: Arbitrum blog

Sources: Decrypt
