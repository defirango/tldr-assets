CoinDCX admits the $16M loss it called a false alarm

CoinDCX said it lost 15.99M USDT from deposit wallets it had retired after its $44M hack in July 2025. Its automated sweeping service failed for a period, so deposits piled up at an old address, per its 25 Oct update. The exchange says it absorbed the loss, user balances are unaffected and trading and withdrawals kept running.

Why it matters. On 21 Oct CEO Sumit Gupta called the first reports "a false alarm", per The Block, and ZachXBT questioned the claim that the wallets were retired. The funds left for one Tron address on 20 Oct, per The Block. Tether froze about 2.21M USDT across nine wallets, per crypto.news. The confirmation was X's top crypto news story on 25 Oct.

If you hold or use this: Using CoinDCX: confirm the latest deposit address in the app before every deposit.

X100 loses $4.85M to a flash-loan Senate takeover

On 24 Oct an attacker flash-borrowed about 1.94M USDT from Aave, bought X and locked it to win two of the three Senate seats that run X100's treasury. In the same transaction the new Senate sold 400,000 treasury X into a pool the attacker had just created, and the treasury got 35.68 USDT, per Crypto Times. About $4.85M in USDT left across two transactions, and a third wallet took about 550,000 X.

Why it matters. Seats went to whoever had the most X locked at that moment, and a proposal could pass and execute in one transaction, per Crypto Times. X fell 96.5% to $1.78 by 17:38 UTC on 24 Oct. The first attacker swapped into about 2,023 ETH across five wallets, and X100 had not commented when Bitquery checked. Grok marks the loss as preliminary, and DefiLlama has not listed it yet.

If you hold or use this: Holding X or providing liquidity in its pools: treat them as compromised (Grok).

Sources: Blockaid on X, Bitquery, The Defiant, Crypto Times

Fearless Wallet leaked private keys

Polkadot warned that anyone who used the Fearless Wallet browser extension since February 2025 should assume their accounts are compromised, per Crypto Briefing. Versions 3.0.1, 3.0.3, 3.0.4 and 3.0.5 sent private keys to an attacker's server on unlock. Eight users reported losing 17,286 DOT.

Why it matters. The warning dates from 21 Oct and is still the advice. Updating the extension does not help, since an attacker may already hold a working key. Fearless is a third-party wallet.

If you hold or use this: Used it: create new keys in a different wallet and move funds now. Ignore anyone offering to recover funds.

Sources: Crypto Briefing

One thing to learn

Flash-loan governance attacks. An attacker borrows capital inside one transaction, uses it to gain temporary voting power, passes a malicious proposal and repays the loan before the block ends. X100 counted locked balances at the moment of the vote and had no timelock, so the seats flipped in the same block. Before you use a protocol, check whether voting uses snapshots and whether proposals wait before they execute (Grok, Crypto Times).
