Argentina drops the LIBRA case against Milei

Federal judge Marcelo Martínez de Giorgi dismissed the criminal case over the LIBRA memecoin against President Javier Milei, his sister Karina, Hayden Davis and eight others, per CoinDesk. He found Milei's post and the token's crash were not enough to prove fraud without evidence of a specific deception.

Why it matters. LIBRA's collapse wiped out over $250M in investor wealth, and wallets tied to its creator pulled about $99M, per CoinDesk. The prosecutor asked for the dismissal. Investors removed as plaintiffs are challenging their removal, so the case may not be fully closed. Watcher.Guru's post drew 539K views.

Sources: Watcher.Guru on X, CoinDesk

Initia names the MoveVM bug behind its $2.7M drain

Initia said an attacker used a bug in MoveVM's script caching at 14:59 UTC on 23 Oct to bypass security checks and credit themselves unbacked WETH, iUSD and USDC. About $2.7M left before the halt: 105 WETH and about 1.4M AUSD0 via LayerZero, plus about 1.08M USDC via Noble and CCTP. It now sits as 1,135.52 ETH in one Ethereum wallet.

Why it matters. No native INIT was minted and total INIT supply is unaffected. Initia expects the fix to be done and audited in a few days, and asset recovery is under way with security partners. The L1 and rollups stay halted until then, with a full post-mortem to follow. Agora said AUSD is fully backed and froze the attacker's address, per Unchained.

If you hold or use this: Holding or using Initia: ignore unofficial links and wait for verified updates.

Sources: Initia on X, Unchained, D2 Finance on X

Mastercard agent payments settle in USDC on Base

Base said Mastercard is adopting an agent-payment method that uses x402 and USDC and settles on Base, per TokenPost. Mastercard showed Agent Pay for Machines on 20 Oct at Cloudflare Connect: a service answers with HTTP 402, and the agent signs a voucher within its approved budget.

Why it matters. Vouchers can be bundled and redeemed later. In the demo, Coinbase Developer Platform funded the agent's wallet and Base handled settlement, per Stablecoin Insider. No volumes or rollout dates were given.

Sources: TokenPost, Stablecoin Insider

One thing to learn

VM-level bugs. On Initia, a bug in MoveVM's script caching let malicious code skip security checks and spoof the VM's state. The attacker credited themselves tokens without changing total supply, so supply checks saw nothing wrong. Those unbacked tokens then pulled real assets out of the bridges before the chain paused. A flaw at the VM layer sits below every app, so it can bypass controls each app relies on (Grok, Initia).
