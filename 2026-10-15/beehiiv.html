PPI prints 6.3% and BTC tags $80k

September PPI rose 1.3% on the month against 0.6% expected, and 6.3% on the year against 5.4%, per BLS data reported by Coinpaper. Core PPI rose 5.0% on the year. It landed a day after the hot 3.7% CPI. Korea's Kospi had already fallen 8% overnight and triggered circuit breakers, per CoinDesk. BTC dipped to $79,806 at 13:55 UTC on CoinGecko, then rallied to $82,247 by 15:30 UTC. About $52M of positions were liquidated in one hour of the bounce, 93% of them shorts, per Coindoo citing CoinGlass.

Why it matters. CME FedWatch odds of an October hike eased to about 26% after jumping to nearly 47% on CPI, per CoinDesk. The Fed meets 27-28 Oct. CoinGlass shows $926M liquidated in 24h with longs at 82%, so most of the leverage was cleared before the bounce.

If you hold or use this: Holding BTC: $79.8k is today's low and the level Grok's trade is built on.

Sources: CoinDesk: Kospi rout, Coinpaper: PPI, BLS PPI, Coindoo: short squeeze, CoinGlass

Starknet polls holders on going independent L1

Starknet opened a non-binding Snapshot poll on 15 Oct on whether to explore becoming an independent post-quantum L1. StarkWare floated the idea on 8 Oct. Starknet's STARK proofs are hash-based and its accounts can adopt post-quantum signatures, but its bridge and data availability still depend on Ethereum, per The Block. A Yes vote starts research and more proposals. No timeline exists. When The Block wrote it up, 177.6M STRK had voted, 99.91% Yes. The same day, the monthly unlock of 127M STRK went to early contributors and investors, about 1.7% of circulating supply, per CryptoTicker.

Why it matters. STRK rose 17.8% in 24h and 55.2% in 7d on CoinGecko. Coindoo notes the trading data does not prove the vote drove the move.

If you hold or use this: Holding STRK: the poll runs seven days. Unlocks continue monthly until March 2027.

Sources: Starknet on X, Snapshot poll, Forum proposal, The Block, CryptoTicker: unlock

Pirate Chain rolls back after an exploit

Pirate Chain found an exploit in its new transparent layer, tied to the Ironwood turnstile that tracks supply between layers. Developers are rolling the chain back to 14 Oct, 1:00 PM Pacific Time. Exchanges have paused ARRR deposits and withdrawals. The team has not given a loss figure and says details follow once the rollback and investigation are done.

Why it matters. Transactions after the rollback point will not exist on the restored chain. Replies to the post asked what happened to code is law.

If you hold or use this: Using ARRR: do not send, receive or open your wallet until the team confirms the rollback is complete.

Sources: Pirate Chain on X, AMBCrypto

Ledger unveils the Atom phone

Ledger revealed Ledger Atom at its Op3n event in Paris. It runs Android next to Ledger OS on an EAL6+ secure element and switches to a Secure Mode to sign. The Founder's Edition costs $849 with a $100 refundable deposit, then $899. Deliveries are expected in summer 2027, pending certification. Tamper sensors run on their own battery, and a Core Protection feature targets screen-reader implants like the one in the CryptoBilis case, per The Block. Solana Mobile's Seeker app and Ledger Agent Intent for AI agents come built in.

Why it matters. Ledger is still investigating the modified wallets sold through CryptoBilis. The launch post had 337.7K views when we read it.

Sources: Ledger on X, The Block

EU names USDC its first significant stablecoin

The European Banking Authority classified USDC as a significant e-money token under MiCA, the first of 45 notified in the EU. From 12 Nov, the EBA directly supervises USDC's own funds, reserves, liquidity and recovery and redemption plans. France's ACPR keeps governance and fit and proper checks. The EBA also signed a memorandum with New York's DFS, which oversees Circle's New York operations.

Why it matters. USDC supply is close to $73B, second to USDT at about $184B, per The Block.

Sources: EBA, The Block

One thing to learn

Why hot inflation hits crypto. A hot CPI or PPI print raises the expected path of short-term rates. Higher real yields compete with assets that pay no yield, and leveraged longs get forced to cover. That is why liquidations cluster on the release even when the price move is modest.
