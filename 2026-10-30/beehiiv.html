News that matters

Binance loses $6.3B in the largest crypto hack ever

From 20:20 UTC on 29 Oct, funds left Binance hot wallets across several chains for about 20 minutes, per Chainalysis. Binance puts the damage at $6.3B. It has reserved over $6.34B of company funds, with $5B already held in the exact tokens taken, and is buying the rest on market and OTC. Withdrawals are paused and deposits stay open. Co-CEO Richard Teng said the attacker used Ethereum's EIP-7702 feature and a self-destruct mechanism, and the restart target is about 40 hours (CoinDesk).

Why it matters. Binance first blamed a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop, then found more breach points. It says there is no evidence wallet private keys were stolen. Its recovery post drew 2.1M views and Yi He's 'User funds are SAFU' 614K. DefiLlama lists it as 2026's largest hack at $6.36B (Grok).

If you hold or use this: Using Binance: wait for the official restart post. Ignore DMs, use only official channels and turn on app 2FA (Grok).

Sources: Binance on X, CoinDesk, Chainalysis, The Block, DefiLlama hacks

$3B frozen as the DPRK trail firms up

Tether froze over $1.567B in USDT and Circle over $800M in USDC (CryptoPotato). Arbitrum's Security Council froze about $30.5M, taking the total frozen above $2.83B by 01:01 UTC, per ZachXBT. Chainalysis now counts about $2.8B frozen and Binance about $3B. Chainalysis and TRM tie the attack to North Korea's TraderTraitor with high confidence.

Why it matters. ZachXBT saw funds from this hack and the $388M Bitget exploit commingled at one address after passing through THORChain. Chainalysis saw the same link. Laundering looks automated, with thousands of transactions and many wallets seeded with exactly 1 TRX.

If you hold or use this: Holding a token the hacker received: watch the issuer's channels for freezes or deposit pauses, as PUNDIX and FUN did.

Sources: ZachXBT on X, Chainalysis, CryptoPotato, Yi He on X (Pundi X freeze)

Intel crash and a tech rout push BTC under $77k

Intel fell 29% on weak Q4 guidance and AI names slid, with the Nasdaq down 3.4% and the S&P 500 down 2.2% by mid-afternoon in New York. BTC fell below $77,000 and was down about 8.4% for October, on track for a second straight red October (CoinDesk).

Why it matters. Crypto stocks took both hits. Coinbase fell 12% after Q3 revenue of $1.22B, down 35% year on year, even as net income rose 66% to $719M on crypto gains (The Block). Strategy fell 11% after its CFO left. Coinbase's BTC premium hit a z-score near -2, a deep discount seen only twice since 2023 (CoinDesk).

If you hold or use this: Holding BTC: crypto is trading with tech today. Watch Nasdaq futures alongside the Binance restart.

Sources: CoinDesk live, The Block (Coinbase), The Block (BTC)

Aave freezes every market, then reopens

At 22:44 UTC on 29 Oct Aave froze all DAO-operated V3 and V4 markets as a precaution, pausing new deposits and borrows while withdrawals and repayments kept working. That covered 175 V3 markets on 16 networks and 117 V4 markets (CryptoPotato). Aave reopened them on 30 Oct.

Why it matters. Founder Stani Kulechov said Aave was not affected. USDT, USDC, XAUT and PAXG temporarily cannot be used as collateral for new loans (CoinDesk).

If you hold or use this: Borrowing on Aave: check which collateral still counts before you open a new loan.

Sources: Aave on X, CoinDesk live, CryptoPotato

Hacker dumps a 2017 token and FUN crashes 90%

The wallet Etherscan labels 'Binance Hot Wallet Exploiter 2' received FUN at 23:28 UTC and sold 134.1M of it, mostly into a Uniswap v2 pool that held about $16,000. It got about 111.5 ETH, roughly $250,000. FUN fell more than 90% in that pool (CoinDesk).

Why it matters. The wallet still holds about 580.8M FUN, roughly 5.4% of max supply. FUN was down 62.6% in 24h on CoinGecko at 20:34 UTC.

If you hold or use this: Holding FUN: the overhang is still in the hacker's wallet. FUNToken asked exchanges to pause deposits.

Sources: CoinDesk

Trezor flags an implanted Safe 5 from CryptoBilis

Trezor got a report on 28 Oct of a Safe 5 with an extra board holding a cellular module and antenna that could read the screen, including the recovery phrase, and send it out. The unit was bought in August via Tokopedia from CryptoBilis, a distributor Trezor dropped earlier in October (The Block).

Why it matters. Trezor treats the report as credible but has not examined the device. Four other devices from the reseller showed no tampering, and no fund losses are reported. It comes a day after Singapore police warned about tampered Ledgers.

If you hold or use this: Bought a Trezor from CryptoBilis since March: move funds to a new wallet and contact Trezor Support for a new device.

Sources: Trezor, The Block

One thing to learn

Hot wallet. A hot wallet is an exchange wallet that stays online so withdrawals can be signed fast. If an attacker gets the signing system to approve transfers, the drain can hit many chains in minutes. Issuers can freeze stablecoins like USDT and USDC. Native BTC and ETH cannot be frozen. A cover fund like SAFU repays users but cannot stop the first outflow (Grok).
