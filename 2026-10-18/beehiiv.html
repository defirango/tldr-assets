News that matters

Chip-stock perps lose $52M in 10 minutes

About $51.6M of leveraged bets on chip stocks and chip ETFs were force-closed between 21:22 and 21:32 UTC on Saturday while US markets were shut, per CoinGlass data in CoinDesk. About $38M of it was longs. SOXL took the biggest hit at about $12.7M, then SanDisk and SK Hynix at about $10.8M each. Tread.fi CEO David Jeong traced the first large sells to Binance at 21:21:59 UTC. Less than a second later, about ten addresses fired sell orders across six chip contracts on Hyperliquid within 1.8 seconds, all opening shorts.

Why it matters. With the stock market closed, these contracts had no live share price to follow, so forced selling fed on itself. Trade XYZ sets its own reference prices until US quotes return on Monday, per CoinDesk. Memory-chip shares were already weak on worries about Chinese rival CXMT.

If you hold or use this: Trading stock perps on weekends: size for thin books. Prices can gap with no live quote behind them.

Sources: David Jeong on X, CoinDesk, Crypto Briefing

Crypto ETFs shed $610.7M in a week

US crypto ETFs lost $610.7M over 12 to 16 Oct, per Farside data compiled by crypto.news. Ether funds led with $320.1M out, ahead of bitcoin funds at $228.4M. BlackRock's ETHA lost $299M and IBIT $165.4M. Solana funds lost $25.5M, Zcash $19.2M and Hyperliquid $17.5M. Friday alone saw $166M leave across all products.

Why it matters. Outflows halved from $1.29B the week before. Crypto Briefing puts October bitcoin ETF outflows near $615M so far. ETH is down 7.1% over 7 days on CoinGecko, against 3.3% for BTC.

If you hold or use this: Holding ETH: ETF demand is the weak spot. Watch Monday's flow print.

Sources: crypto.news, Crypto Briefing

Nine companies block their tokens on OKXICE

OKXICE, the OKX and Intercontinental Exchange venue, posted a notice listing nine companies that objected to tokenized trading of their shares: Cerebras, AppLovin, Applied Digital, Rocket Lab, CoreWeave, Verizon, Applied Optoelectronics, Broadcom and UnitedHealth, per The Block. Under the SEC's innovation exemption, a timely objection bars that venue from listing the company's token. Eight of the nine were among the 63 tickers OKXICE named on 4 Oct.

Why it matters. OKXICE told the WSJ it still plans to list dozens of names, including Nvidia, Apple and Tesla. Nasdaq said it will opt out for its own shares. Tokenized stocks are worth about $3.5B, up from about $680M at the start of the year, per The Block.

Sources: The Block

Cysic bridge exploited for 10M CYS

An attacker got into a database behind Cysic's cross-chain bridge and pushed a fake mint message, minting 10M CYS on BNB Chain at 12:31 UTC on 17 Oct, per Cysic's incident report. Cysic blacklisted the address at 13:47 UTC, freezing about 2.8M CYS. The attacker had already sold 7.2M CYS and bridged about 306.6 ETH, roughly $727K, to Ethereum, per The Block.

Why it matters. Cysic says its mainnet and existing user balances were untouched. The bridge stays closed while a security firm traces the funds, and the final loss figure is still pending.

If you hold or use this: Holding CYS: stay off the bridge until Cysic reopens it and posts the follow-up report.

Sources: Cysic on X, The Block

Monero leads a privacy rotation

XMR rose 4.8% to $587 on CoinGecko, one of only 22 gainers among 113 coins above $500M cap. Coinotag reads it as a spot-led move on rotation into privacy coins, with no coin-specific catalyst. Coinpaper logged an intraday high near $608, and AMBCrypto put XMR open interest at $261M, per KuCoin.

Why it matters. U.Today points to supply leaving exchanges as more platforms delist XMR, and reports the EU is pushing licensed exchanges to phase it out by 2027. It also notes price has run ahead of usage.

If you hold or use this: Holding XMR: delistings cut both ways. Thinner exchange supply can squeeze price and also make exits harder.

Sources: Coinotag, U.Today, Coinpaper, KuCoin

One thing to learn

Funding rates. Funding on perpetuals shows who pays whom to hold a position. Positive funding means longs pay shorts, which points to bullish positioning. Mildly positive rates with flat prices often mean crowded longs waiting for a catalyst that may never come.
