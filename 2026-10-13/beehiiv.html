News that matters

MediaMarkt pulls Ledger as the spy chip details land

Ledger's Donjon team says the implant in CryptoBilis units sits inside the enclosure, reads the 24-word phrase off the screen during setup and sends it over LTE with a provisioned eSIM. It leaves the Secure Element, the OS and signatures untouched. Every confirmed loss still traces to CryptoBilis. On 13 October MediaMarkt Saturn stopped selling Ledger devices in stores and online after a customer in Germany reported an implant. Ledger says no funds were lost in that case. Onchain researchers put losses at more than $86M (Specter) and up to $93.2M. Ledger has not verified either figure. Trezor says there is no evidence its devices were compromised and will end reseller authorizations on 1 Jan 2027 as a precaution.

Why it matters. The scare now reaches a major European retailer, and Ledger says it is reviewing reports of implants in other signers and locations. The total lost and the number of victims are still unconfirmed.

If you hold or use this: Bought from CryptoBilis: do not set it up, or move funds to a new Ledger-bought device with a new seed. Lost funds: talk to law enforcement before opening the device. Bought from MediaMarkt: contact Ledger Support.

Sources: Ledger on X: MediaMarkt, Ledger on X: Donjon update, The Block, CoinDesk, Decrypt, Trezor on X

Blur shuts its frontend on 12 Nov

Blur says the blur.io frontend will be deprecated on 12 November. The marketplace contracts stay live onchain, and BLUR keeps control of the fee switch. Blend stops new loans, and borrowers have until 12 Nov to repay or let loans default. A separate withdrawal tool comes after. Monthly volume fell from about $1.43B in March 2023 to under $7M by March 2026, per CoinDesk. Blast, built by the same team, announced its shutdown on 2 Oct. BLUR is down 35.7% in 24h on CoinGecko.

Why it matters. Blur held over 70% of Ethereum NFT volume at its 2023 peak, per CoinDesk. Its exit shows how far NFT trading has shrunk.

If you hold or use this: Repay Blend loans before 12 Nov or they default. Watch for Blur's withdrawal tool.

Sources: Blur on X, The Block, CoinDesk

South Korea blocks 27 offshore exchange websites

South Korea's FIU said the communications regulator ordered blocks on the websites of 27 unregistered offshore exchanges, including KuCoin, MEXC, BingX, Phemex and BitMart, after reviews on 6 and 7 October. It extends the 2025 block on their apps. The FIU has referred 44 unregistered operators to investigators and will chase mirror domains. Only 28 providers are registered.

Why it matters. Regulators can now request a block as soon as they spot unregistered activity, with no court ruling needed, per CoinDesk.

If you hold or use this: If you trade from Korea on one of these venues, withdraw. The FIU says DAXA will help where access breaks.

Sources: Wu Blockchain on X, Chosunbiz, CoinDesk, FIU notice

Binance closes its mining pool on 10 Nov

Binance will discontinue Binance Pool on 10 November at 07:00 UTC, along with Pool Savings, Cloud Mining and the Transaction Accelerator. Shares sent after the cutoff are not credited. Payout steps close at 23:59 UTC that day, and the pool pages go dark on 24 November. Binance cites an ongoing review of its products.

Why it matters. The Block notes Binance is pushing into TradFi perps, US stock and ETF options and AI trading tools. Miners on the pool have four weeks to move.

If you hold or use this: Point miners at another pool before 10 Nov, 07:00 UTC, redeem Pool Savings and save your records.

Sources: Binance on X, Binance notice, The Block

Strive bought 3,311 BTC between 5 and 9 October at about $84,500 each, its largest buy since January. It now holds 32,773 BTC, per its SEC filing. The Block puts it 11,227 BTC behind Metaplanet for second place among public holders.

Why it matters. Strive's CEO wants the No. 2 spot by year-end. The Block works out that needs about 990 BTC a week.

Sources: The Block, SEC 8-K

One thing to learn

Frontend shutdown. When a project turns off its website and leaves its smart contracts live, users can still reach the contracts through other interfaces or direct calls. Liquidity and ease of use usually collapse once the official frontend goes. Blur's contracts and BLUR fee switch stay live after 12 November.
