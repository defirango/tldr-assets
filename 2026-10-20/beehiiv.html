News that matters

BlackRock files PBIT, a buffer bitcoin ETF

BlackRock filed with the SEC on 19 Oct for the iShares Bitcoin Managed Protection ETF, ticker PBIT. The fund holds no bitcoin. It buys and sells IBIT call options and holds fixed income across three tranches with staggered monthly starts. Each tranche protects from about 90% of the reference value, so losses stop near 10% before fees. The upside cap is set at each tranche's start, per Decrypt.

Why it matters. IBIT took in $665.2M on 19 Oct, its biggest day since October 2025, while FBTC lost $83.6M and GBTC $54.9M. Spot BTC ETFs netted $526.6M. Calamos, First Trust and Innovator already run buffered BTC funds.

Sources: Decrypt, Seyffart on X, The Block (filing), The Block (IBIT flows)

Drift exploiter moves $61.6M into Tornado Cash

A wallet tied to Drift's 1 April hack deposited 25,700 ETH, worth $61.56M, into Tornado Cash over two hours on 20 Oct after two months of inactivity, per Lookonchain. It still holds 81,864 ETH, about $195M.

Why it matters. Drift reported about $295M in losses, and a Mandiant investigation attributed the attack to North Korea's UNC6862, per Cointelegraph. Drift opened DFX recovery-token claims on 1 Oct with a pool of about $3.1M. DefiLlama ranks it the third-largest hack of 2026.

Sources: Lookonchain on X, Cointelegraph, Drift DFX claims

France votes a 31.4% tax on stablecoin swaps

The National Assembly adopted amendment I-3898 to the 2027 budget on 16 Oct, 38 votes to 37 with 25 abstentions, per CoinDesk. It ends tax deferral on crypto swaps into MiCA e-money tokens, so moving into a stablecoin would trigger the 31.4% capital gains rate from 1 Jan 2027.

Why it matters. It is not law yet and can still be changed or removed. Public Accounts Minister David Amiel asked for its withdrawal. Chainalysis warned it could push traders to DeFi, P2P and offshore venues.

If you hold or use this: Trading from France: swaps into stablecoins may become taxable from 1 Jan 2027. Watch the remaining budget stages.

Sources: CoinDesk, Watcher.Guru on X

Switzerland approves its first retail spot BTC ETF

FINMA approved a fund that holds bitcoin directly under Swiss fund rules and can be sold to retail investors, announced 20 Oct. It waived its usual diversification rules, citing bitcoin's long trading history and volumes. FINMA did not name the issuer.

Why it matters. Existing Swiss bitcoin ETPs are debt securities. This is the first bitcoin product under the investment fund framework, and other providers have applied, per The Block.

Sources: The Block, FINMA, Watcher.Guru on X

CME starts trading UNI and BCH futures

FalconX and GSR executed the first block trade in CME's new Uniswap and Bitcoin Cash futures, CME said on 20 Oct. UNI is the first DeFi token with CME futures, per Grok.

Why it matters. CME's crypto futures and options average over 242,000 contracts a day in 2026, about $7.9B notional. UNI joins BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL, ADA, LINK, XLM, AVAX and SUI on the venue.

Sources: CME Group

Aztec Connect attacker exits into Tornado Cash

The attacker behind the June exploit of deprecated Aztec Connect contracts deposited 1,255.8 ETH, about $3.01M, into Tornado Cash on 19 Oct in 30 transactions, per Salus via ChainCatcher. The original haul was about $2.19M.

Why it matters. The attacker first redeemed Yearn vault tokens for another 346.8 ETH. All six stolen ERC-20 balances are now empty.

Sources: KuCoin News, ChainCatcher

One thing to learn

Buffer ETFs. Buffer ETFs like PBIT use options spreads to limit losses in exchange for a cap on gains. PBIT holds IBIT options and fixed income and holds no bitcoin directly, so any effect on spot demand runs through how dealers hedge those options.
